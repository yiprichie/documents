# Hook Skits: Learn Spanish by Doomscrolling

Format: 15-25s vertical (9:16) skits. They play in English, and one to three Spanish words or phrases get taught by context, never by a lecture. Each skit has a hook in the first 2 seconds and a joke or twist at the end.

## The teaching trick (use in every skit)
1. **Say it:** a character uses the Spanish word naturally.
2. **Context clue:** the other character's reaction or a visual makes the meaning obvious.
3. **Payoff:** the word comes back at the end as the punchline, so it sticks.
4. On-screen text shows `word = meaning` once, at the payoff, and a recap card closes the clip.

## Recurring cast (keeps video models consistent)
- **Mia:** 20s, curly dark hair, yellow hoodie. Learner, chaotic.
- **Abuela Rosa:** 70s, grey bun, floral apron, deadpan. Teaches by being savage.
- **Diego:** 20s, barista, green apron, flirty. Mia's crush.

## 1. "Abuela Judges Your Order" (food)
**Teaches:** `la cuenta` (the bill), `con hielo` (with ice)
**Hook (0-2s):** Abuela stares into camera: "You ordered it *wrong*."

| Time | Scene | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Close-up of Abuela, arms crossed at a café table | ABUELA: "You ordered it wrong." |
| 2-7s | Mia holds a lukewarm coffee | MIA: "It's lukewarm!" ABUELA: "You said it with no ice. You must say *con hielo*." |
| 7-13s | Diego brings an iced coffee, Mia sips happily | MIA: "Con hielo... con hielo! Okay, that's good." |
| 13-20s | Abuela slides a long receipt over | ABUELA: "*La cuenta*." MIA: "What?" ABUELA: "The bill. Con hielo costs extra." |

Text overlay at the end: `con hielo = with ice` · `la cuenta = the bill`

## 2. "Mia Says the Wrong Word" (embarrassing mistakes)
**Teaches:** `estoy embarazada` is NOT "I'm embarrassed"; correct: `tengo vergüenza`
**Hook:** Mia, to a stranger at a party: "I'm so embarazada right now."

| Time | Scene | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Party, Mia sweating, talking to a guy | MIA (proud): "I'm so *embarazada* right now!" |
| 3-8s | The guy freezes, drops his drink | GUY: "Congratulations?!" |
| 8-14s | Mia panics, Abuela pops up from behind a plant | ABUELA: "*Embarazada* means pregnant, mija." |
| 14-20s | Mia face-palms | MIA: "Then how do I say embarrassed?" ABUELA: "*Tengo vergüenza.* Say it now." MIA (small voice): "...tengo vergüenza." |

Text: `embarazada = pregnant (not embarrassed!)` · `tengo vergüenza = I'm embarrassed`

**Video prompt:** "Vertical 9:16 comedy skit, house party with warm string lights. Young woman, curly dark hair, yellow hoodie, proudly says something; a man freezes mid-sip and drops his drink. Cut to an elderly woman with grey bun emerging from behind a large houseplant, deadpan. Quick snap zooms, exaggerated reactions, handheld."

## 3. "Date Night Panic" (flirting)
**Teaches:** `guapo` (handsome), `¿Cómo te llamas?` (what's your name?)
**Hook:** Mia hides behind the menu: "He's so *guapo*, I'm going to die."

| Time | Scene | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Mia peeks over a menu at Diego | MIA (whispering to Abuela): "He's so *guapo*." |
| 3-8s | Abuela, unimpressed | ABUELA: "Guapo? He put milk in my espresso." MIA: "Still *guapo*." |
| 8-14s | Diego walks over, Mia freezes | DIEGO: "Hi. ¿Cómo te llamas?" MIA: "Um... yes?" |
| 14-20s | Abuela whispers the answer | ABUELA: "He asked your name." MIA (to Diego): "Me llamo... Mia. Mia guapa." ABUELA: "That's *you* calling you handsome." |

Text: `guapo = handsome` · `¿Cómo te llamas? = what's your name?`

**Video prompt:** "Vertical 9:16 rom-com skit in a sunny café. Young woman in a yellow hoodie hides behind a menu, peeking at a charming barista in a green apron. Elderly woman with grey bun beside her whispers, deadpan. Barista walks over smiling; the woman freezes with wide eyes. Close-ups on faces, quick cuts, handheld phone look."

## 4. "The Taxi Driver Speed-Run" (travel)
**Teaches:** `más despacio` (slower), `a la derecha` (to the right)
**Hook:** A taxi driver goes 150 km/h and Mia screams: "SLOWER!"

| Time | Scene | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Back seat, city blurring past | MIA: "Slower! Slower! SLOWER!" DRIVER: "Qué?" |
| 3-8s | Abuela is in the front seat, calm | ABUELA: "*Más despacio.* Say it." MIA: "Más despacio!" The car slows. |
| 8-14s | Mia relieved, then sees a wrong turn | MIA: "Wait, turn right!" DRIVER (looks confused) |
| 14-20s | Abuela, sipping tea | ABUELA: "*A la derecha.*" MIA: "A la derecha!" Car turns, perfect parking. DRIVER: "Gracias." |

Text: `más despacio = slower` · `a la derecha = to the right`

**Video prompt:** "Vertical 9:16 comedy skit inside a taxi, daytime Spanish city streets blurring fast. Terrified young woman in a yellow hoodie grips the back seat. An elderly woman with a grey bun sits calmly in the front passenger seat sipping tea. Dashboard-cam angle, fast motion blur, then a smooth stop. Exaggerated expressions, realistic."

## 5. "Abuela's Fridge Audit" (home, quick vocab)
**Teaches:** `tengo hambre` (I'm hungry), `la nevera` (the fridge)
**Hook:** Mia opens an empty fridge and whispers: "Where's all my food?"

| Time | Scene | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Mia stares into an empty fridge | MIA: "Where's all my food?" |
| 3-8s | Abuela appears behind her holding a plate | ABUELA: "In *la nevera*? No. In my stomach." |
| 8-14s | Mia pouts | MIA: "But I'm hungry!" ABUELA: "Say it right: *tengo hambre.*" |
| 14-20s | Mia repeats it, Abuela slides over a plate of empanadas | MIA: "Tengo hambre." ABUELA: "Better." (hands her the plate, steals one back) |

Text: `la nevera = the fridge` · `tengo hambre = I'm hungry`

## 6. "The Worst Apology" (social)
**Teaches:** `lo siento` (I'm sorry), `no pasa nada` (it's okay / no problem)
**Hook:** Mia spills coffee on a stranger: "I'm SO sorry!"

| Time | Scene | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Mia spills coffee on a man's shirt | MIA: "I'm so sorry!" |
| 3-8s | Man, calm | MAN: "*No pasa nada.*" MIA: "What does that mean?" |
| 8-14s | Abuela walks in | ABUELA: "It means it's okay. And when YOU spill, you say *lo siento.*" |
| 14-20s | Mia spills again | MIA: "Lo siento!" MAN: "No pasa nada." ABUELA (dry): "The third one is expensive." |

Text: `lo siento = I'm sorry` · `no pasa nada = it's okay`

**Video prompt:** "Vertical 9:16 comedy skit in a busy café. Clumsy young woman in a yellow hoodie spills coffee on a calm man's white shirt. Elderly grey-bun woman in a floral apron walks in with a flat expression. Quick cuts, comedic timing, slow-motion spill, realistic."

## Production notes
- **Character consistency:** generate a reference image of each character once, then feed it as the reference for every video model call. Keep the outfit identical.
- **Voices:** generate English lines and Spanish words with a Spanish-capable TTS voice per character, so the Spanish words are pronounced correctly.
- **Pacing:** TikTok-style cuts every 2-3s, captions on screen the whole time, loopable endings.
- **Quality gate:** have a native Spanish speaker check every script before rendering.
