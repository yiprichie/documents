# Reel template library for Richard's personal brand

Owner: Richard | Writer: Jack | Written: 2026-11-03 | Status: DRAFT, nothing filmed or posted

Why this exists: the first three story scripts all came back in the same shape (hook, pain, struggle x2, turning point, overcame, honest close; 7 beats; same rhythm). This library gives 10 clearly different shapes. Each one changes the number of beats, the opening, the pacing and the look.

## Rules that apply to every template (Richard's rules)
- 30 seconds MAX. One punchy line per beat. Catchy, makes people feel something.
- Every Reel is about pain, struggle and how Richard overcame it (or honestly has not yet). The shape changes, the substance does not.
- NEVER invent personal facts, names, quotes, numbers, results or events. Real details are `[RICHARD: ...]` slots. If a slot is not true for him, change the line. No claimed success he has not had.
- Friends, Gen Z coworkers, classmates and interviewees stay anonymous: no names, faces, voices, handles, workplaces or schools. Richard checks he is comfortable.
- Only Richard's own footage. On-screen text is equal to or shorter than the spoken line.
- Jack cannot film, edit or post. Richard picks licensed audio in the Instagram app.
- The first line of a template replaces the old default order (hook, pain, struggle, turning point, overcame, close). Use the beat order of the chosen template.

## 1. POV

- **When to use:** one raw moment, one feeling. Great for travel low points, first day nerves, a startup "what have I done" second. Use when the story is a feeling, not an explanation.
- **Emotional effect:** instant empathy, "that's me". Quiet and a little ache.
- **Format:** TEXT-ONLY on screen over B-roll. No talking. Optional soft music. Fastest to film.
- **Length:** 15-20s (well under 30s). 3 beats.
- **Hook formula:** `POV: [RICHARD: a specific uncomfortable moment, in second person "you"]`

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-5s | POV card | "POV: you are [situation]." (held shot, no cut) |
| 2 | 5-13s | The feeling | "[The one thing going through your head.]" |
| 3 | 13-20s | The turn | "[The small thing you did / still do.] [Honest tag.]" |

Examples:
- **Travel:** 1. "POV: [RICHARD: a moment you were alone and something went wrong]." 2. "[RICHARD: what you thought in that second]." 3. "[RICHARD: the small thing you did next.]"
- **Studying at 40:** 1. "POV: you are [RICHARD: age] and the youngest person in the room is [RICHARD: their rough age, no names]." 2. "[RICHARD: the thought you hid.]" 3. "[RICHARD: what you did anyway.]"
- **Startup:** 1. "POV: [RICHARD: the moment before a hard step, e.g. about to send, ask, launch]." 2. "[RICHARD: what your stomach said.]" 3. "[RICHARD: you did it / you waited, honestly.]"

## 2. Myth vs Reality

- **When to use:** Richard is correcting a belief (his own or other people's) such as "you're too old", "travel is a waste", "startups are glamorous".
- **Emotional effect:** a small jolt of recognition, then relief. Slightly rebellious.
- **Format:** TEXT-ONLY on screen over B-roll (white text on dark clip). A spoken DIRECT version works too.
- **Length:** 24-28s. 4 beats.
- **Hook formula:** `MYTH: [common belief people said or he believed].` (card, hard cut, then REALITY)

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-5s | MYTH card | "MYTH: [belief]." (big letters, no footage yet) |
| 2 | 5-12s | REALITY | "REALITY: [what is actually true for you]." |
| 3 | 12-21s | Proof | "[One real moment that proves it. No names.]" |
| 4 | 21-28s | Honest catch | "[What is still hard / the part the myth got half right.]" |

Examples:
- **Travel:** MYTH: "[RICHARD: belief, e.g. travel is only for the rich or the young]." REALITY: "[RICHARD: your truth]." Proof: "[RICHARD: one moment]." Catch: "[RICHARD: the cost or doubt that is real.]"
- **Studying at 40:** MYTH: "[RICHARD: belief, e.g. school is for kids]." REALITY: "[RICHARD: what you found]." Proof: "[RICHARD: one class moment, no names]." Catch: "[RICHARD: what is still hard.]"
- **Startup:** MYTH: "[RICHARD: belief, e.g. founders just know]." REALITY: "[RICHARD: how it feels]." Proof: "[RICHARD: one real day or task]." Catch: "[RICHARD: what you still do not know.]"

## 3. Cliffhanger, Part 1 and Part 2

- **When to use:** a story with a real outcome Richard already knows, where waiting makes people come back. Only use when the Part 2 answer is true and ready. Post Part 2 within 24-48 hours.
- **Emotional effect:** suspense, curiosity, a "wait, what happened?" pull. Two Reels, two moods.
- **Format:** DIRECT-TO-CAMERA or B-ROLL voiceover. Part 2 can switch format for contrast.
- **Length:** about 20s per Part. 4 beats each (8 total).
- **Hook formula (Part 1):** `[Stakes line]. [Cut off before the answer.]` / **(Part 2):** `Yesterday I left you at [moment].`

**Part 1**

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-4s | Stakes | "[Something was on the line.]" |
| 2 | 4-10s | Setup | "[Where he was, what he wanted.]" |
| 3 | 10-16s | Escalation | "[The moment it got worse.]" |
| 4 | 16-20s | Cut-off | "And then... (Part 2 tomorrow.)" End on a freeze frame. |

**Part 2**

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-3s | Rewind | "Yesterday I left you at [moment]." |
| 2 | 3-9s | The turn | "[What happened next.]" |
| 3 | 9-15s | What I did | "[His real choice.]" |
| 4 | 15-20s | Honest close | "[What it taught him / what is still open.]" |

Examples (Part 1 beats 1-4 shown; Part 2 follows the same slots):
- **Travel:** 1. "[RICHARD: what was at stake on one trip]." 2. "[RICHARD: where you were]." 3. "[RICHARD: what went wrong]." 4. "And then... Part 2 tomorrow."
- **Studying at 40:** 1. "[RICHARD: a deadline, exam or presentation at stake]." 2. "[RICHARD: how you prepared]." 3. "[RICHARD: the moment it slipped]." 4. "And then... Part 2 tomorrow."
- **Startup:** 1. "[RICHARD: a decision that could change the project]." 2. "[RICHARD: what you were choosing between]." 3. "[RICHARD: what made it hard]." 4. "And then... Part 2 tomorrow."

## 4. Then vs Now

- **When to use:** visible change over time: a place, a habit, a skill, a state of mind. Needs two real moments of footage.
- **Emotional effect:** pride without bragging, then the honest "but". Hopeful.
- **Format:** B-ROLL, A/B cuts or a split screen. Voiceover. Opens on NOW, freezes, then rewinds.
- **Length:** 28s. 5 beats.
- **Hook formula:** `This is me now. This is me [RICHARD: time] ago.` (start on the NOW clip)

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-4s | NOW freeze | "This is [now image]." (freeze frame, record scratch cut) |
| 2 | 4-10s | Rewind to THEN | "[Time] ago, I was [then image]." |
| 3 | 10-16s | THEN pain | "[What it felt like / what I feared.]" |
| 4 | 16-22s | The bridge | "[The one thing that changed.]" |
| 5 | 22-28s | NOW, honest | "Now [result]. [Not yet / still hard.]" |

Examples:
- **Travel:** 1. "This is [RICHARD: a place you stood recently]." 2. "[RICHARD: time] ago I was too scared to go." 3. "[RICHARD: the fear]." 4. "[RICHARD: what changed]." 5. "[RICHARD: what is different, and what still scares you.]"
- **Studying at 40:** 1. "This is my desk today." 2. "[RICHARD: time] ago I had not opened a book." 3. "[RICHARD: the doubt]." 4. "[RICHARD: the habit that changed]." 5. "[RICHARD: how it feels, and what is not solved.]"
- **Startup:** 1. "This is [RICHARD: where the project is today]." 2. "[RICHARD: time] ago it was [RICHARD: the starting point]." 3. "[RICHARD: the pain of the start]." 4. "[RICHARD: the thing that moved it]." 5. "[RICHARD: honest status, no hype.]"

## 5. Three Things Nobody Told Me (Countdown)

- **When to use:** Richard has three distinct lessons from the same experience. Good for "what I wish I knew".
- **Emotional effect:** useful and saveable, with a gut-punch at #1. Rhythmic.
- **Format:** HYBRID. Direct hook, B-roll or text cards for each number, direct close. (Direct-only also works.)
- **Length:** 28s. 5 beats.
- **Hook formula:** `[Big "3" on screen] 3 things nobody told me about [experience].`

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-4s | Number hook | "3 things nobody told me about [experience]." (huge "3") |
| 2 | 4-11s | Thing 3 | "Number 3: [small, light truth]." |
| 3 | 11-18s | Thing 2 | "Number 2: [harder truth]." |
| 4 | 18-25s | Thing 1 | "Number 1: [the truth that hurt most]." (slow down, close framing) |
| 5 | 25-28s | Tag | "[One line: still worth it / still unsure.]" |

Examples:
- **Travel:** 1. "3 things nobody told me about travelling [RICHARD: young / alone / on a budget]." 2-4. "[RICHARD: three real lessons, lightest to heaviest]." 5. "[RICHARD: tag.]"
- **Studying at 40:** 1. "3 things nobody told me about going back to school at 40." 2-4. "[RICHARD: three real things, no names]." 5. "[RICHARD: tag.]"
- **Startup:** 1. "3 things nobody told me about building a startup." 2-4. "[RICHARD: three real things]." 5. "[RICHARD: tag.]"

## 6. The Confession

- **When to use:** Richard was wrong about something and paid for it. Needs a real mistake or false belief.
- **Emotional effect:** vulnerability and trust. Disarming. People forgive honest mistakes.
- **Format:** DIRECT-TO-CAMERA, close framing, plain wall. Almost no B-roll.
- **Length:** 30s. 6 beats.
- **Hook formula:** `I was wrong about [thing]. And it cost me [RICHARD: what].`

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-3s | The confession | "I was wrong about [thing]." |
| 2 | 3-8s | What I believed | "I thought [old belief]." |
| 3 | 8-14s | The moment I found out | "Then [what happened that exposed it]." |
| 4 | 14-19s | The cost | "It cost me [what]." |
| 5 | 19-25s | What I do now | "Now I [new behaviour, only if true]." |
| 6 | 25-30s | Honest rest | "[What I still get wrong.]" |

Examples:
- **Travel:** 1. "I was wrong about [RICHARD: a travel belief]." 2. "I thought [RICHARD]." 3. "Then [RICHARD: the trip moment]." 4. "[RICHARD: the cost]." 5. "[RICHARD: what you do now]." 6. "[RICHARD: what you still get wrong.]"
- **Studying at 40:** same pattern with "[RICHARD: belief about studying or being older]".
- **Startup:** same pattern with "[RICHARD: a real startup assumption]".

## 7. Reply to a Doubt

- **When to use:** a real comment, message or doubt (from a friend or a viewer) that stings or sparks a story. Only real ones, paraphrased and anonymous.
- **Emotional effect:** conversation, defensiveness turning into openness. Feels like a response, not a broadcast.
- **Format:** HYBRID. Text of the doubt on screen, Richard direct for reaction and answer, B-roll in the middle as evidence.
- **Length:** 30s. 6 beats.
- **Hook formula:** `[On-screen doubt in quotes.] ...Yeah. Let me answer that.`

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-3s | The doubt | On-screen: "[The real doubt, paraphrased, no name]" |
| 2 | 3-8s | The sting | (direct) "[Honest first reaction.]" |
| 3 | 8-14s | What they don't see | (B-roll) "[The hidden part of the story.]" |
| 4 | 14-20s | The evidence | (B-roll) "[One real moment.]" |
| 5 | 20-26s | The answer | (direct) "[His honest answer in one line.]" |
| 6 | 26-30s | Thank / open door | "[Thanks, or a question back.]" |

Examples:
- **Travel:** Doubt: "[RICHARD: a friend's or viewer's real question, e.g. is travel worth it while young?]" then the beats above.
- **Studying at 40:** Doubt: "[RICHARD: a real comment about age or a career change]".
- **Startup:** Doubt: "[RICHARD: a real doubt about the startup or your age or timing]".

- **Emotional effect:** tender, nostalgic, a lump in the throat. Slow.
- **Format:** B-ROLL with voiceover. Opens on his own handwriting (his hand, a notebook). Cut to relevant own footage per line. Gentle piano or no music.
- **Length:** 30s. 7 beats.
- **Hook formula:** `Dear [RICHARD: age] me,` (handwritten, voiceover reads it)

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-3s | Salutation | "Dear [age] me," |

| 3 | 7-12s | It will hurt | "[The thing that will be hard.]" |
| 4 | 12-16s | It will take longer | "[The thing that takes longer than hoped.]" |
| 5 | 16-21s | But | "But [one real good moment]." |
| 6 | 21-26s | The advice | "[What he would tell him to do.]" |
| 7 | 26-30s | Sign-off | "[Honest sign-off, e.g. still figuring it out.] - Me." |

Examples:

## 9. One Breath Monologue

- **When to use:** a fresh, same-day, emotional thing (like the interview day) where energy matters more than polish. Spontaneous and funny or raw.
- **Emotional effect:** urgency, intimacy, "he's telling me right now". Breathless, then a sudden stop.
- **Format:** DIRECT-TO-CAMERA, one handheld take, NO cuts. Walking or pacing. Fast.
- **Length:** 28-30s. 8 short lines, about 3.5s each, no pauses between them.
- **Hook formula:** `Okay, so. [what just happened]` (start already talking, no intro)

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-3s | Mid-motion open | "Okay, so [what just happened]." |
| 2 | 3-7s | The odd detail | "And the funny/strange thing is [detail]." |
| 3 | 7-10s | What I expected | "I walked in thinking [expectation]." |
| 4 | 10-14s | The fear | "Honestly, I was scared of [fear]." |
| 5 | 14-17s | The swerve | "And then [what happened]." |
| 6 | 17-21s | The meaning | "Which means [lesson]." |
| 7 | 21-25s | The change | "So tomorrow I'm [action]." |
| 8 | 25-30s | The sudden stop | "[Short honest tag]." Cut hard. |

Examples:
- **Travel:** "Okay, so I just got back from [RICHARD: place / moment]..." and slots.
- **Studying at 40:** "Okay, so I just left class and [RICHARD: what happened]..."
- **Startup:** "Okay, so today I [RICHARD: what happened in the startup]..."

## 10. Day-in-the-Life Timeline

- **When to use:** to show the grind or the rhythm: study days, a build day, a travel day. Needs real clips from a real day.
- **Emotional effect:** respect and recognition. "I see the work." Builds slowly, ends on one honest line.
- **Format:** B-ROLL with clock stamps (06:10, 08:45...) as on-screen text. Short voice lines or text only. Fast cuts.
- **Length:** 30s. 9 beats of about 3s.
- **Hook formula:** `06:10. [first honest line of the day]`

| # | Time | Beat | Fill-in line pattern |
|---|------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 0-3s | First stamp | "[time] [how the day starts]." |
| 2 | 3-6s | Morning | "[time] [the obligation]." |
| 3 | 6-9s | Commute / transition | "[time] [the in-between]." |
| 4 | 9-12s | Work / class | "[time] [the main effort]." |
| 5 | 12-15s | The wall | "[time] [the hardest moment]." |
| 6 | 15-18s | The small win | "[time] [a small real thing that helped]." |
| 7 | 18-22s | Evening | "[time] [the second shift]." |
| 8 | 22-26s | Late | "[time] [the quiet doubt]." |
| 9 | 26-30s | Honest close | "[time] [tomorrow, same again / why]." |

Examples:
- **Travel:** a travel day: "[RICHARD: time] [wake / transit / arrival / first meal / lost / found / night / close]".
- **Studying at 40:** "[RICHARD: times and real moments of your study day]".
- **Startup:** "[RICHARD: times and real moments of a build day]".
