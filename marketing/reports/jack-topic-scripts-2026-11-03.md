## Script 1: Travel. A solo day trip on a small budget (Day-in-the-Life Timeline, 9 beats, B-ROLL, 30s)

Look and pace: fast cuts, a clock stamp on every clip, short voice lines (or text only). Sets up tension in the first three beats, then reports.

Pre-filming checklist (do these first):
- [ ] Pick a place you have never been, reachable in a day.
- [ ] Set a budget limit and write it in your notes app. Only spend what you can genuinely afford.
- [ ] Tell one person where you are going and when you will be back.
- [ ] Write down, before you leave, what scares you about it. That is beat 3.
- [ ] Screenshot nothing with card numbers, tickets with barcodes or booking codes.

Shot list (own footage only): door and shoes; notes app; timetable or window reflection; arrival sign; street; hands with map or cup; food; dusk window; door again. 9 clips of 2-4 seconds each, each with a clock stamp.

Questions: Why this place? What is your real budget? What scared you, and when did it show up (or did it not)? Are you comfortable showing the location?

## Script 2: Studying at 40. Day 1 back in class (POV, 3 beats, text-only over B-roll, 18s)

Look and pace: quiet, one held shot per beat, big white text, almost no cuts. A different feel from Script 1: slow and still, no voice.

Pre-filming checklist (do these first):
- [ ] Find the entrance the day before, or arrive 15 minutes early.
- [ ] Check whether you may film outside or in corridors. Never film inside a class with people in it.
- [ ] Decide whether you are comfortable sharing your age and the subject. If not, drop "[age]".
- [ ] Write the one thought in your head at the door. That is beat 2.
- [ ] Avoid school names, signs, badges, timetables or screens in the frame.

## Script 3: Startup. Show my idea to 3 real people (Three Things Nobody Told Me, 5 beats, DIRECT-TO-CAMERA, 28s)

Look and pace: plain wall or desk, chest-up, eye level. The hook is filmed BEFORE the three conversations. The three reports and close are filmed after. Count down, slow on number 1. A different feel from Scripts 1 and 2: spoken, rhythmic, no clips.

Pre-filming checklist (do these first):
- [ ] Book 3 real people for 10 minutes each. If you only get 2, change the number everywhere.
- [ ] Ask each one: "Is it OK if I share, anonymously, what you say?"
- [ ] Do not film or record them. Take notes after.
- [ ] Write your honest fear before the first talk. That is beat 1.
- [ ] If your idea is confidential, decide how vague to be. No project name needed.
- [ ] Quote no one word for word unless they said yes. Paraphrase.

| # | Time | Spoken line (to camera) | On-screen text | Delivery | Filmed |
|---|------|-------------------------|----------------|----------|--------|
| 1 | 0-4s | "Three people. Ten minutes each. I'm about to tell them my idea, and I'm scared of [RICHARD: your real fear]." | Giant "3", then "10 minutes each" | Steady, eye contact | Before |
| 2 | 4-11s | "Number 3: [RICHARD: the lightest thing one of them said, paraphrased, no names]." | "3. [RICHARD: shorter]" | Light, half smile | After |
| 3 | 11-18s | "Number 2: [RICHARD: the harder thing someone said, paraphrased]." | "2. [RICHARD: shorter]" | Slower | After |
| 4 | 18-25s | "Number 1: [RICHARD: the one that stung or surprised most, honestly]." | "1. [RICHARD: shorter]" | Slowest, closer to the lens | After |
| 5 | 25-28s | "[RICHARD: honest tag: what you do next, or that you are still unsure.]" | [RICHARD: shorter] | Plain, no hype | After |

Caption: "I told my idea to 3 real people, 10 minutes each. I was scared of [RICHARD: fear]. Here's what I heard. [RICHARD: one honest sentence.] What would you ask yourself?"
Hashtags: #startup #buildinginpublic #founderjourney #feedback #personalstory

Audio direction: voice only, no music, or very light background music (check it does not cover your voice). Richard picks licensed audio in the Instagram app. Film in one take per number; do beat 1 first, before you talk to anyone.

Shot list (own footage only): you to camera for all five beats against the same plain wall or desk. Optional: 2-3 seconds of your notes (no names, no private data) under beat 4.

Questions: Which 3 people (just a type, like "a friend", "a stranger", no names)? What was your fear? What did each say? What do you change now? Are all three OK with you sharing, anonymously?
