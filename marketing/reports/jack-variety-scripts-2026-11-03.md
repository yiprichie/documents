## Script 3: Startup. One Breath Monologue (8 beats, DIRECT, one take, no cuts, 30s)

Look and pace: handheld, close, walking or pacing, no pauses, lines tumble into each other, sudden stop at the end. Filmed the same day as the interview if possible.

| # | Time | Spoken line (to camera) | On-screen text | Delivery |
|---|------|-------------------------|----------------|----------|
| 1 | 0-3s | "Okay, so, today I interviewed people for my startup." | "Today I interviewed people." | Already talking as the clip starts |
| 2 | 3-7s | "And the funny thing was... [RICHARD: the funny thing]." | "The funny thing was..." | Half laugh |
| 3 | 7-10s | "I walked in thinking [RICHARD: what you expected]." | [RICHARD: shorter] | Quick, no pause |
| 4 | 10-14s | "Honestly, I was scared of [RICHARD: your fear]." | [RICHARD: shorter] | Voice drops a little |
| 5 | 14-17s | "And then [RICHARD: what happened]." | [RICHARD: shorter] | Speed up |
| 6 | 17-21s | "Which means [RICHARD: what you learned]." | [RICHARD: shorter] | Look right at the lens |
| 7 | 21-25s | "So tomorrow I'm [RICHARD: what you will change]." | [RICHARD: shorter] | Steady |
| 8 | 25-30s | "No idea if it works. Wanted you to hear it today." | "Day by day." | Stop mid-stride, hold 1s |

Caption: "Interviewed people for my startup today. The funny part: [RICHARD: the funny thing]. More as it happens."
Hashtags: #startup #buildinginpublic #founderjourney #storytime #entrepreneur

To film: one continuous handheld take, 3-4 attempts. Do not film the interview or the interviewees. Keep it to your own face and walk.
Questions: What was the funny thing (not told yet)? What did you expect and fear? What actually happened? What did you learn and change? Can you say the project name, or keep it vague?
