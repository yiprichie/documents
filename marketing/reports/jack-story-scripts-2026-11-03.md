## Batch-filming tips (per script)
- Script 1 (B-roll): scroll your own camera roll first; film only the missing clips (a ticket, a window, a street) in one 20-minute walk. Record the voiceover in one quiet take.
- Script 2 (direct-to-camera): film Scripts 2 and 3 in the same sitting, same desk and light; do each line as a separate short take so cutting to 3-5s beats is easy.
- Script 3: film it the same day as the interview while the reaction is fresh; record two takes of the first line.

No invented quotes.
