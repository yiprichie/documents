# John's poster style (from Richard's reference)

Reference image: `marketing/assets/john-poster-reference.webp` (GO TOKYO "11月東京幾度？" poster).

## Format
- Portrait 4:5, 1080 x 1350 px (Facebook / Instagram feed). Traditional Chinese text.

## Layout, top to bottom
1. Full-bleed seasonal background (reference: autumn leaves in orange, red, yellow). Match the season/topic of the item: leaves for autumn, sakura for spring, snow for winter, etc. Use Canva stock or generated art, never someone else's photo.
2. Rounded white info box (thin orange outline, slightly translucent) holding a bold dark-green title and a thin orange divider, then a two-column bulleted list with orange dots. 4-8 short items.
3. Big hero headline in white, heavy weight, with a dark-green outline and soft shadow (reference: "11月東京幾度？"). A question or punchy hook, about 10 characters.
4. Key number(s) line in green, large and bold (reference: high/low temperatures). One glanceable fact: price in NT$, a deadline, a temperature or a date.
5. Dark green pill banner with white text: one practical tip or call to action.
6. Small credit line at the bottom centre: 資料來源：<site>.

## Palette (taken from the reference)
- Dark green (headline outline, title, pill): approx #1F4D33
- Bright green (key numbers): approx #1E8E4E
- Autumn orange (bullets, box outline, sparkles): approx #E8692A
- White text on green; warm cream/pink gradient fades at the bottom
Adjust accent colors to the season, keeping dark green for text and the pill.

## Rules
- Use only facts Joe verified from pages he read. Every number on the poster must trace to a source in Joe's report. Never invent.
- Keep text short and large enough to read on a phone. No emoji.
- Always include the credit line. If Joe flagged a caveat (sponsored, last year's dates, indicative fare), keep it off the poster but pass it to Richard in the caption notes.
- Deliver the design as a Canva link plus an exported PNG, and the matching caption.
