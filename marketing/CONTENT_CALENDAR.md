## Format definitions
- B-ROLL: voiceover or on-screen text lines over his own footage. A short shot list is given.
- DIRECT-TO-CAMERA: Richard speaks each one-liner to the lens. Setting, framing and tone are noted.
- HYBRID: direct-to-camera hook and close, B-roll in the middle.
