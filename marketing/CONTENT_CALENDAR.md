## Assumptions (please confirm)
- 3 Reels per week (one per theme), 4 weeks, starting Monday 2026-11-09. Mon = Travel, Wed = Studying at 40, Fri = Startup. The 4 topics per theme run across the 4 weeks in the order Richard gave them. He can move them.
- Script length: 30 seconds MAX per Reel. Single punchy lines, one per beat, each cutting to the next. No paragraphs. On-screen text is equal to or shorter than the spoken line.
- Each Reel uses a shape from `marketing/REEL_TEMPLATES.md`. All 10 templates are used. No template repeats back to back (checked in order). Only two repeats exist, because 12 entries need more than 10 templates: Three Things (entries 3 and 10) and POV (entries 2 and 12), each 7+ slots apart.
- Hooks are written to work BEFORE the outcome is known ("I'm doing X today and I'm scared of Y"), so Richard can film the setup first and the result later. Every entry has a "Do this before filming" step: one small real action. If he does not do it, the Reel is not filmed.
- Language: English (confirmed). Richard is OK appearing on camera (confirmed).
- Friends, coworkers, classmates, strangers and travellers stay anonymous: no names, faces, voices, handles, schools or workplaces. Where Richard talks to people (entries 3, 5, 10, 12), he asks their OK before sharing even a paraphrase, does not film or record them, and checks he is comfortable.
- Money and safety: entries 1, 4 and 7 involve spending. Only do what is genuinely affordable and safe; do not book or spend just for the Reel. If he decides not to book (entry 4), the honest story is that, and the script changes.
- Nothing here is a claim about Richard's life until he answers the `[RICHARD: ...]` prompts. If an attempt does not happen or goes differently, change the line. Never claim success he has not had.
- Jack cannot film, edit or post. Richard shoots and publishes, and picks licensed audio in the Instagram app.
- Brand colours, fonts and logo are still blank in BRAND.md, so no Canva covers yet.

## Format definitions
- B-ROLL: voiceover or on-screen text lines over his own footage.
- DIRECT-TO-CAMERA: Richard speaks each one-liner to the lens.
- HYBRID: direct-to-camera hook and close, B-roll in the middle.

## Batch-filming tips (one per week)
The action comes first, the filming second. Film the "before" clips (setup, fear, plan) right before the action, and the outcome clips right after, then record voiceovers in one quiet sitting.
- Week 1: Film entry 1's door, ticket and fear lines at departure; film the rest of the day as you go. Film entry 2's door and corridor clips before class. Film entry 3's hook before the three talks, and all three reports to camera in one sitting after.
- Week 2: Film entry 4 Part 1 right before the click and Part 2 within 48 hours. Film entry 5 after asking. Film entry 6's hook at the start of the 48 hours and the rest after shipping.
- Week 3: Film entry 7 at the table and B-roll on the walk home. Write entry 8's letter by hand the same day as the class or meeting. Film entry 9 only after you have your replies (or the silence).
- Week 4: Film entry 10's B-roll on the days you ask, and record the direct hook and close after. Keep the daily log clips for entry 11 (15 seconds a day is enough). Film entry 12's text-only clips on the same day you say it out loud.
